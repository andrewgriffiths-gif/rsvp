Install on your iPhone

For the best experience — fullscreen, with its own home-screen icon and offline support — install this app to your home screen.

1. Tap the Share button in Safari (the square with the arrow ↑). 2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. 3. Tap Add in the top right.

Open it from your home screen any time after that. Books, covers and progress are kept on this device.
